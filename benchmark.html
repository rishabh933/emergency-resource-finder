Brute-force vs. Quadtree vs. K-D Tree (nearest-neighbor queries)

Generates synthetic datasets of increasing size within Gwalior's bounding box, builds a Quadtree and a K-D Tree for each size, and runs 20 repeated random nearest-neighbor queries against the hospital subset, averaging the nodes-visited and time-taken for each approach. This implements the benchmarking methodology from Milestone 2.
